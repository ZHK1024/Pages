2. Service Description

Lock Screen Todo is a productivity app that displays your system reminders as a live wallpaper on your lock screen. You can trigger wallpaper generation via Shortcuts to get an at-a-glance view of your current tasks — no unlocking required. The app supports multiple task filter scenes (such as Today, Overdue, Upcoming, Flagged, and more) as well as wallpaper templates (the Minimal Todo template is currently available, with more on the way), letting you personalize how your task list is displayed.
