Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 30, 2026

The short version: Lock Screen Todo does not collect, store, or upload any of your personal data. Your privacy is our top priority.

1. Overview

Welcome to Lock Screen Todo! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Lock Screen Todo is a productivity app that displays your to-do items as a live wallpaper on your lock screen. It reads your system reminders and uses Shortcuts to generate a personalized lock screen wallpaper, letting you stay on top of your tasks without ever unlocking your device. We are committed to protecting your privacy and have implemented the highest level of data protection.

2. Permissions

Lock Screen Todo only requests the following permission, and only after you have explicitly granted access:

  • Reminders: Used to read your system reminders in order to generate lock screen wallpaper content. The app will never modify, delete, or upload your reminder data.

We do not request sensitive permissions unrelated to core functionality, such as location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

3. Information We Don't Collect

Lock Screen Todo does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following types of data to our servers:

  • Personal identification information (e.g., name, email address, phone number)
  • Device information (e.g., device model, operating system version)
  • Location data
  • Usage statistics or analytics data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Your reminder content, wallpaper configurations, or any user-generated content

4. Data Storage

All data generated while using Lock Screen Todo, including but not limited to:

  • Wallpaper template configurations and scene settings
  • App preferences
  • Any other user-generated content

This data is stored locally on your device only and will never be uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers.

5. Data Deletion

When you uninstall Lock Screen Todo, all locally stored data will be completely removed, leaving no trace on your device.

6. Third-Party Services

Lock Screen Todo does not use any third-party analytics, advertising services, or other SDKs that could collect user data. All frameworks used by the app are provided by Apple (EventKit, SwiftData, StoreKit, AppIntents, etc.).

7. Network Access

Lock Screen Todo may request network access, but only for the following purposes:

  • Content Delivery: Downloading and loading tutorials, help documentation, or updated configuration data
  • Purchase Verification: Verifying your purchase receipts via Apple StoreKit

Rest assured, we will never upload your personal data, reminder content, or wallpaper configurations to our servers or any third-party servers.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. In fact, we do not collect personal information from users of any age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We recommend checking back periodically for the latest information.

10. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any data, there is no server-side data for you to access, modify, or delete. You have full control over all data stored on your device.
